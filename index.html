The Verbatim Rule

By the rules of this portal, the highest-voted post MUST be read aloud — word for word, exactly as its author wrote it — and discussed, by a representative of the United States, in front of the entire country. Another post, drawn at random, must be read and discussed the same way.

AI will be used to detect profanity and errors, correct grammar, and suggest clearer formatting. It will never steer you: your topic, your position, or your meaning. And once your post is published, it is final.

Keep in mind that your post may be read in front of the entire nation. Write in your most professional manner. Profanity, slang, and ridicule will not post. State your idea or concern plainly and professionally. You are not posting a comment. You are addressing the nation — speaking to and for every American. Give it the seriousness it deserves.

Fine print (official register)

All submissions are subject to automated pre-publication review. Submissions containing profanity, slurs, or personal attacks shall not be published. Submissions containing slang or errors materially affecting comprehensibility shall be held pending correction by the submitting party, with suggested revisions provided for consideration; no submission shall be altered by the System without the submitting party’s express action. Weekly selections shall consist of (i) the submission receiving the highest number of votes during the applicable period and (ii) one (1) submission selected by random drawing from among all eligible published submissions. Selected submissions shall be read aloud in their entirety, verbatim, and discussed in the weekly briefing. A published submission is final and shall not be edited or redacted.

Statements of policy, principles, and fundamentals

The purpose of this platform is to ensure that every citizen has the opportunity to be heard by the nation. Citizens will address the nation and speak on its behalf.

  • The highest-voted post and one randomly drawn post are read aloud word-for-word, in full, by a U.S. representative and discussed in the weekly briefing. No editor stands between author and nation. Nothing is edited after posting.
  • AI detects problems and suggests wording before publication only. It never steers a post's topic, position, or meaning, and never changes, substitutes, or declines a post on its own.
  • Profanity, slurs, and personal attacks are hard-blocked and never publish. Slang, misspellings, grammar, and unclear text are held with suggested rewording; the author accepts or retypes, and AI never silently changes text. Held drafts are not published, do not enter the draw, and do not use the citizen's one-post slot.
  • Once published, a post is final. It cannot be deleted, withdrawn, archived, or hidden.
  • Voting is upvote-only. One vote per citizen per weekly cycle, switchable until voting closes Sunday at 11:59 PM ET.
  • One post per citizen per weekly cycle.
  • Posts are capped at 630 words.
  • The random draw is rig-proof: the eligible pool is frozen in a fixed published order at the cutoff, and the position is the NIST Randomness Beacon value mod pool size, plus one. The draw is internal only, with no button, replay, or re-roll.
  • The committee prepares answers only. It never alters, substitutes, delays, or declines a selection. It reviews selections a week ahead and routes each answer to the correct branch and level.
  • The committee does not fund. It asks for funds and allocates funds.
  • Committee answers start as AI drafts, reviewed, edited, and approved by a real committee member who is the answerer of record. AI never delivers a final answer alone and never touches a citizen post. The public sees only approved answers.
  • An exact-duplicate post in the same cycle does not create a new post; the citizen is offered an upvote on the existing post, with no post-anyway exit. The draw pool excludes the winner and any post exactly matching the winner. Similar-but-distinct posts are advisory only.
  • The Live Feed shows the current cycle only. Past-cycle posts that were never selected are deleted; selected posts are kept as the briefing record.
  • Posting and voting run Monday 12:00 AM to Sunday 11:59 PM ET. The pool freezes at the Sunday cutoff, the draw is Monday at 12:00 PM ET, the committee has the week plus a Monday-Tuesday buffer, and the briefing airs Wednesdays at 1:00 PM ET as the fixed opening segment.
  • This Week shows only the most recently aired briefing. Next Week shows the next briefing's selections. A briefing's replay publishes when that briefing airs. Prior Posts & Answers and Status Updates list aired selections only.
  • Participation is for verified citizens. The demo uses simulated verification only and never collects or stores real identification.
